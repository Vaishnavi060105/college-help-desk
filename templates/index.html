🔬 6 Advanced NLP Architecture Pillars:

1. Dense Vector RAG: Cosine similarity over institutional sub-documents to prevent hallucinations.

2. MemAC Conversational Memory: Stateful tracking of student branch, category, and previous turns.

3. IOB BIO Sequence Tagging: Extracts named entities like B-BRANCH, B-CATEGORY.

4. Code-Switching Index (CMI): Calculates intra-sentential Marathi/English mix percentages.

5. Urgency & Sentiment Triage: Flags payment failures and urgent student queries.

6. Marathi Speech Engine: gTTS natural spoken Devanagari synthesis with audio player.
