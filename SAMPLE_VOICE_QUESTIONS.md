# 🎙️ Sample Questions to Speak into the College Help Desk Assistant

Use these exact sentences when testing or demonstrating your project in front of your teacher/guide. They are written in natural, conversational Romanized Marathi/Hindi code-mixed with English (*Maranglish*).

---

## 🌟 Top 3 Best Questions for Quick Demonstration

1. **Admission Inquiry:**
   > *"Bhai first year engineering admission kuthun apply karu?"*
   - **Intent:** `admission_query`
   - **Answer spoken:** Explains CET Cell process and ARC verification timings.

2. **Fees Inquiry:**
   > *"College fee installment madhe pay karta yete ka sanga na?"*
   - **Intent:** `fee_query`
   - **Answer spoken:** Explains online ERP portal payment and installment requests in accounts.

3. **Placement Inquiry:**
   > *"Campus placement madhe highest package kiti gela ya year la?"*
   - **Intent:** `placement_query`
   - **Answer spoken:** Explains TPO statistics, average 5.5 LPA, highest 18 LPA.

## 💡 Demonstration Tips
1. Speak clearly at normal conversational speed into the microphone.
2. In the Streamlit web app (`http://localhost:8501`), click the microphone icon under the **"🎙️ Ask by Voice"** tab, speak your sentence, and click stop.
3. Review the text in the confirmation box, click **"✅ Confirm & Get Answer"**, and listen to the voice answer in Marathi!
